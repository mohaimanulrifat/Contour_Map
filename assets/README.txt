Branding
--------
Put your logo here as  logo.png  (any size; it is scaled to fit the header, transparent PNG
recommended). Set your app name in contourmap/branding.py (APP_NAME).
Optional: replace ../contour.ico to change the window/exe icon.

fonts/  Inter (SIL Open Font License, see fonts/Inter-LICENSE.txt) - loaded privately by the app
        on Windows; falls back to Segoe UI if it cannot be loaded.
